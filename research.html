Research library

Three reports I could trace to their publishers. Each one connects back to the chapter.

Child Labour: Global estimates 2024, trends and the road forward

Organisation
International Labour Organization (ILO) and UNICEF
Year
2025 (data for 2024)
Topic
How many children are in child labour worldwide, and how that has changed
Main finding
Nearly 138 million children were in child labour in 2024, about 54 million in hazardous work, down by more than 20 million since 2020, but the world missed the 2025 target.
Link to the chapter
