What do we think?

After reading the chapter, I asked people around me six questions. The charts below show what they said, and only that.

The results

The questions

  1. Before reading Lost Spring, how aware were you of slum living conditions?
  2. Do you think child labour is still common in India today?
  3. What is the biggest reason children leave school to work?
  4. Have you ever seen a child working near where you live or travel?
  5. Should child-labour laws be enforced more strictly?
  6. Which single step would help children most?

How to add the results

Open js/survey-data.js, set respondents to the total number of people who answered, and put the real count against each option. Save, refresh, and the bar charts, pie charts and percentage cards appear on their own.

Until real numbers are entered, this page shows “Survey data will be added here”. The optional sample preview uses invented numbers and is marked clearly on screen, so it cannot be mistaken for findings.

Tip for the write-up: state who you asked, how many people, when, and how you chose them. A small survey of classmates is not the same as a national one, and it is fine to say so.
