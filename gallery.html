Images and drawings

The vector drawings were made for this project. The larger pictures were supplied by the project author; add their creator or tool in each caption. Real photographs can be added in the marked frames, with a caption, source and permission.

To add a photograph, copy one of the figure blocks in gallery.html, save the image in assets/images/, and use <img loading="lazy" alt="…"> with meaningful alt text. Do not use photos from Google Images unless the licence allows it.
