LOST SPRING

The title suggests a childhood that is deprived of freedom, education, happiness and opportunities.

Scroll through the seasons

Spring

Green, open, and full of things about to grow.

Fading

The colour drains out when a child has no time or place to grow.

Smoke and furnaces

Work replaces play. This is the dark, industrial side of the story.

Green shoots

Hope returns in small ways: a school, a skill, a chance.

How I read the title

Spring is when things begin. Seeds open, animals are born, the whole world seems to say “start”. Childhood is the same for people: it is when we begin to learn, play and imagine.

In the chapter, that season is “lost” because Saheb and Mukesh are asked to work before they have had time to grow. The word lost also fits the promises that were made and never kept, like the school in Saheb’s story.

The last part of my animation, the green shoots, is my own addition. The chapter is honest about how hard things are, but it does not say there is no hope. Mukesh’s dream and Saheb’s wish to learn are the shoots.

Interpretation is the student’s own. Source text: Anees Jung, “Lost Spring” (NCERT Flamingo).
